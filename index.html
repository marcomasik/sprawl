      How it works

      • The town builds roads and homes by itself.
      • Requests appear at its edges. Build the matching service within 3 tiles before the ring runs out.
      • Every answered request pulls growth toward it. Ignored ones make families leave that side.
      • Every service type covering a home adds a level: houses grow into blocks and towers, up to 7 levels.
      • Drag or WASD to pan, scroll to zoom, Q / E to rotate. Click the minimap to jump.

      Your settlement grows on its own. Answer its requests at the edges and it grows toward your help.
